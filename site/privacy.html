1. Summary

Wouah Mail never sees your email. The app connects directly from your phone to your email provider’s servers (Google for Gmail, or your provider’s IMAP/SMTP server). No email, contact or credential passes through, is copied to, or is analysed on any server run by the developer. The developer has no technical means to read your messages.

  • Your data stays on your phone and with your email provider.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train artificial intelligence models.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.
  • Signing out of an account revokes access and erases all of its data from the phone.

This policy explains in detail what data the Wouah Mail application (“the app”) processes, why, where it is kept, with whom it is exchanged, and how you stay in control. It also covers this website (section 9).

2. Who we are

Wouah Mail is developed and published by Flamme WASSANGOU, an independent developer based in the Republic of the Congo (“the developer”, “we”).

For any question about this policy or your data: flammewassangou@gmail.com.

Because your email is processed only on your device, you remain in control of it. Your email provider (Google, Yahoo, Orange…) remains responsible for the data it hosts, under its own privacy policy, for example the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How the app works

Wouah Mail is an email client, like the Gmail or Outlook apps: it displays and sends the email of an account you already own. It does not provide email addresses and does not host your email.

  • Gmail account: you sign in with Google’s official sign-in (OAuth). The app never sees your Google password; Google gives it an access token limited to the permissions you approve.
  • Other email (IMAP/SMTP): you enter your address and password (or app password); the app uses them to connect directly to your provider’s servers.
  • Instant delivery: to notify you as soon as an email arrives, the app may keep a connection open from your phone to your email provider (an Android foreground service shown by a persistent notification). This connection links your phone directly to your provider, with no intermediary.

The developer operates no server that receives, relays or stores users’ email, contacts or credentials.

6. How data is used

The data above is used exclusively to:

  • display, search, organise and send your email;
  • notify you when an email arrives, according to your settings (full content, sender only or hidden; per-sender ringtone);
  • display, download, share or save attachments, at your request;
  • unsubscribe you from a mailing list, at your request;
  • protect your privacy: blocking remote images (tracking pixels), warning about misleading links, biometric app lock.

Your data is not used for any other purpose.

7. Storage and security

  • On your phone only. Email, contacts, drafts and settings are stored in the app’s private storage (local database), which other apps cannot access.
  • Encrypted credentials. Google tokens and IMAP/SMTP passwords are kept in Android’s secure storage (encryption backed by the device Keystore).
  • Encrypted connections. Communications with Google and email servers use encrypted connections (HTTPS, TLS/SSL).
  • Optional lock. You can require your fingerprint or face to open the app.
  • Backups. App data follows your Android device’s backup rules; encrypted credentials cannot be restored on another device.

8. Sharing and processors

The developer does not share your data with anyone and uses no processor to handle the app’s data. The app’s only network exchanges take place, from your phone, with:

  • Google (Gmail, contacts, account sign-in), if you connect Gmail;
  • your email provider’s servers (IMAP/SMTP), if you connect another address;
  • senders’ websites, only when you ask: opening a link, loading the images of an email, an unsubscribe page;
  • Google Play, for installing and updating the app, under Google’s rules.

If a future feature ever required a server (for example a notification relay), it would be optional, described in this policy before it goes live, and would never receive the content of your email.

9. This website

This website (wouah-mail.flamme.work) presents the app and its legal documents. It is separate from the app: the app sends it no data.

  • Hosting: a virtual private server rented from Contabo GmbH (Germany). This server only serves the pages of this website; it does not receive, store or process any email, contact or credential of app users.
  • Network: website traffic goes through Cloudflare (protection and performance).
  • Technical logs: like any website, the server and Cloudflare may temporarily record the IP address, date, requested page and browser, for security and reliability. These logs are not used to identify you or for advertising.
  • No cookies, no forms, no analytics and no advertising on this website.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your phone for as long as the account is connected in the app.
  • Signing out of an account (Settings › Accounts) revokes Wouah Mail’s access with Google and erases from the phone all email, contacts, drafts and credentials of that account. Your email remains intact with your provider.
  • Uninstalling the app erases all of its data from the device.
  • You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The attachment cache can be cleared at any time (Settings › Attachments and storage).
  • As the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to retain or delete on our side. For any question, write to flammewassangou@gmail.com.

12. Your rights

Under applicable law, including Congolese Law No. 29-2019 of October 10, 2019 on the protection of personal data and, for people in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability.

Because your data is kept on your device and with your email provider, you exercise them directly: by viewing, editing or deleting your email, signing out, or uninstalling the app. For any request or question, write to flammewassangou@gmail.com; we reply within one month. You may also contact the data protection authority of your country.
